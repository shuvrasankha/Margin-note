## Full description (4000 max)

```
Margin is a notes app that lets you write first and organise later. Open it, type, and your note is saved. Folders, tags, pins and reminders are there when you want them, and out of the way when you don't.

Your notes stay on your phone. There are no ads, no accounts to make and no tracking. Backup is optional and goes to your own Google Drive or another storage place you pick.

WRITE
• Rich notes: headings, quotes, monospaced text, bulleted, dashed and numbered lists, and checklists
• A font and a paper for each note: ruled, squares, graph or four-line
• Templates for meetings, lectures, lab notes, recipes, trips and daily plans, or save your own
• Link notes with [[ and see every note that links back
• Spelling and grammar check as you type, offline
• Find and replace, and version history to go back to an earlier copy

MATH AS YOU TYPE
• End a line with = and the answer appears: sums, percentages, powers, roots, trig and logs
• Name values (rent = 1200) and use them on the lines below, or solve an equation
• Plot graphs from your note and add them as pictures
• A math keyboard with √, π, ^ and °

ADD ANYTHING
• Photos, files and PDFs, with a photo editor to crop and rotate
• Drawings with pens, colours and an eraser
• Voice notes with a waveform
• Tables, web link previews and a document scanner
• Text from photos and handwriting to text, both on your phone

PLAN YOUR DAYS
• To-dos: every checklist in one place, with your progress
• Reminders on any note, once or on repeat, with an optional alarm, and you can add them to your phone's calendar
• A journal with a note for each day, behind your passcode
• Habits with streaks, and trackers like "Books read"

ORGANISE
• Folders inside folders, smart folders, pins and #tags
• Search titles, note text and the text inside photos and PDFs
• Recently Deleted, so nothing is gone by mistake

PRIVATE
• Lock notes with a passcode. Locked notes are encrypted on your phone and in your backups
• Lock the whole app with your passcode or fingerprint
• A password vault with a generator and a strength check

ASK AI (OPTIONAL)
Summarise, rewrite, translate or ask about a note with your own key from Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Groq or NVIDIA. It's off until you add a key, and locked notes are never sent.

SYNC, BACKUP AND SHARING
• Optional Google Drive sync between your phones
• Password-sealed backups to any cloud folder or a WebDAV server
• Import from Google Keep, Evernote, Markdown, text and HTML
• Export everything as Markdown
• Share a note as a styled image, text, PDF or Markdown, or print it

AROUND YOUR PHONE
• Home screen widget, a Quick Settings tile and app shortcuts
• Light and dark themes, and a side-by-side layout on tablets

Privacy policy: https://shuvrasankha.github.io/Margin-note/privacy.html
```

## Graphics

| Asset | File | Play requirement |
|---|---|---|
| App icon | `store/icon-512.png` | 512 × 512 PNG, up to 1 MB |
| Feature graphic | `store/feature-graphic-1024x500.png` | 1024 × 500 PNG or JPG |
| Phone screenshots | `store/screenshots/phone-1.png` … `phone-6.png` | 2 to 8, 1080 × 1920 (9:16) |

Upload the screenshots in order. To change them, edit `store/source/make_screenshots.py` and run `python3 store/source/make_screenshots.py` (it needs Google Chrome). The raw captures it uses are in `store/source/raw/`. They were taken on an emulator with demo notes, not on a personal phone.

Optional: 7-inch and 10-inch tablet screenshots. The app has a side-by-side tablet layout, so adding tablet shots helps it show up as tablet-ready. Play doesn't require them.
