Write first. Organise later.

Margin is a fast, private notes app for Android. Your notes live on your phone, and back up to your own Google Drive when you turn it on.

  • Notes and checklistsTitles, formatting, to-dos, tables, tags and links between notes.
  • Photos, scans and PDFsScan documents, read and edit PDFs, and search the text inside them.
  • Drawing and voiceSketch, write by hand, do quick maths and record voice notes.
  • RemindersGet reminded about a note at the right time.
  • Private by defaultLock notes with a passcode. No ads, no tracking, no account with us.
  • Your own backupSync uses a hidden folder in your Google Drive that only Margin can see.
