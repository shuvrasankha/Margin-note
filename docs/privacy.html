Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Margin ("the app") is a notes app for Android made by Shuvrasankha Paul ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your notes stay on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. We don't run servers that store your notes, we don't show ads, and we don't track you.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put in the app is stored in the app's private storage on your phone: notes, checklists, tables, drawings, photos, scans, PDFs, voice recordings, reminders and settings. This also includes data the app makes for you, such as earlier versions of your notes and the text it reads from your photos and PDFs so you can search them. We can't see any of it.

Locked notes are encrypted on your phone with a key made from your passcode. We never see your passcode.

Google sign-in and Drive backup

Backup is optional and off until you turn it on. When you sign in with Google, the app receives your Google account's email address and name, which it uses to show which account is backing up. It then asks for permission to use one hidden app-only folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). The app can't see or change any of your other Drive files, and the folder doesn't appear in the Drive app.

Your notes and attachments are copied to that folder and kept in sync between your phones. They go directly between your phone and Google; they don't pass through us. Locked notes stay encrypted in your Drive. Google's handling of your Drive data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

You can sign out at any time in the app's settings. To delete the backup, remove Margin's data from Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps in Drive on the web).

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The Drive data is used only to back up and sync your notes, and is never sold, used for ads or shared with anyone.

Features that use the internet

FeatureWhat is sent, and to whom
Drive backup (optional)Your notes and attachments, to your own Google Drive, as described above.
Web linksWhen you add a web link, the app loads that page to show its title, description and picture. The website sees an ordinary request from your phone, like a browser visit.
Cloud grammar check (optional, off by default)If you turn it on, the text of the note you are editing (never locked notes) is sent to LanguageTool to find grammar mistakes. The basic grammar check works offline and sends nothing.
Text recognition, document scanner, handwritingThese run on your phone using Google's ML Kit and Google Play services. Your photos, scans and handwriting are not uploaded. Google Play services may download the models these features need, and ML Kit may send Google basic usage and performance information, as described in ML Kit's terms.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record voice notes, only when you start a recording. A notification shows while it's recording.
  • Notifications: for reminders and the recording controls.
  • Alarms and reminders: so reminders ring on time.
  • Internet: for the features listed above.

Photos and scans come from your camera app, the system file picker or Google's document scanner, and the app only receives the pictures you choose.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect analytics, crash reports or advertising IDs.
  • We don't sell or share your information.
  • We don't have an account system or a server that stores your notes.

Sharing and exporting

When you share or export a note, it goes where you send it (another app, a file, a printer). What happens next is up to that app or person.

Deleting your data

Delete notes in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything on your phone. If you used Drive backup, also remove Margin's data from your Google Drive as described above.

Children

Margin is a general notes app and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
